Investigation runbook

Support-owned until proven engineering-level

01 · Capture and correlate

Exact timestamps and timezone, screenshots, user/device build, socket close codes, client logs, proxy/VPN status, call IDs, and control/audio connection IDs.

02 · Controlled network tests

Coordinate with IT to compare the same device under approved VPN/no-VPN conditions; inspect DNS, proxy and SSL inspection, idle policies and WebSocket upgrade handling.

03 · Escalation boundaries

Escalate reproducible product defects, multi-user signals, unexplained backend closes, or server-side anomalies to Engineering with a concise timestamped evidence bundle.

04 · Verify resolution

Test multiple representative calls and template renderings over a meaningful observation period. Confirm audio/control health, recording state, IT changes, and customer signoff.
