Required take-home response

Five-part technical assessment

1 · Observations

Alex reports a reconnect banner, missing on-screen templates and temporary improvement after reboot. Three audio-forwarding ping/pong monitor timeouts occur at 12:33:06, 13:48:39 and 16:12:38 UTC. A control socket closes and reconnects around 12:47 UTC; a duplicate-socket warning follows at 13:52. Visible START_CALL entries at 16:05 and 16:09 confirm some call initiation succeeds. Later CHECK_START_CALL entries without visible START_CALL do not conclusively establish recording failure.

2 · Possible causes

VPN, firewall/proxy inspection, idle handling, device power or security policies, client runtime stalls, reconnect logic and service-side issues are competing hypotheses. Throughput of 70 Mbps in each direction does not prove stable long-lived connections. The explicit errors concern audio; the missing templates concern control delivery. A common cause has not been proven.

3 · Next investigation

Customer: incident timestamps and timezone, screenshots, OS/device details, exact installer error and representative call IDs. IT: VPN configuration, endpoint security, proxy/WebSocket rules and an approved controlled comparison. Engineering: correlate socket IDs, ping/pong deadlines, close codes, control-path delivery, recording outcomes, and other affected users.

4 · Resolution and verification

Reproduce under controlled conditions, document the changed variable and rollback, test multiple representative calls, confirm reliable templates and expected recording outcomes, monitor both persistent channels and obtain customer confirmation before closure.

5 · Customer update

“We’ve confirmed intermittent connection interruptions in the logs, including repeated audio-channel timeouts. We haven’t confirmed the underlying cause or whether the missing templates share the same trigger. We’re reviewing both connections and coordinating an approved network/VPN comparison with your IT team. Please send the exact reinstall error and two recent occurrence times with your timezone. I’ll provide the next update within one business day, even if the investigation is still open.”

Update cadence is proposed, not asserted as a Balto SLA. Log times are UTC. No definitive diagnosis is claimed.

What Balto actually does

Balto presents Agent Assist, QA Automation, Agentic Insights, Voice Agents, Omnichannel AI, Coaching and Notes as complementary parts of its contact-center AI platform. Its public integrations include CCaaS/contact-center providers and enterprise knowledge tools. This project is an educational simulation of selected workflows—not a product clone or an endorsed implementation.

Dynamic playbook prompts and knowledge-supported suggestions to improve real-time conversations.

QA / Quality management

Interaction review and automated scoring to expand quality visibility.

Agentic Insights

Patterns from customer interactions to inform business decisions.

Voice Agents

AI voice workflows for selected customer requests, with human handoff where appropriate.

Coaching and notes

Evidence-supported performance improvement and conversation summaries.
